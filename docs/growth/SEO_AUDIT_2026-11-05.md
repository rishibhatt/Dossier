# SEO, AI SEO, pSEO and CRO audit, 2026-11-05

Skills run: seo-audit, ai-seo, programmatic-seo, copywriting, cro. Method: crawled all 173 sitemap URLs on the live site (title, meta description, canonical, H1, JSON-LD, word count), read the page code, and compared against the 7-day Search Console numbers the owner shared (59 clicks, 592 impressions, mostly brand queries).

## Executive summary

The site is technically healthy. Every sitemap URL returns 200 from cache in under 1.1 s, has one H1, a self-canonical and no stray noindex. Robots allows all crawlers, including AI search bots. llms.txt is complete and current.

Views are low because the domain is about four weeks old and almost all ranking content is generic ("how to write a resume", "resume action verbs") where large sites own page one. Search Console shows Google tests the site at positions 30 to 90 for those. The clicks that do arrive come from brand searches and narrow country/format posts (academic CV, German CV, UK CV), where competition is thin.

So the fix is focus: write posts where the searcher has a specific, high-intent need that big generic sites answer poorly (India freshers, migration CVs, placement resumes), and make every page convert the few visitors it gets.

## Still open (not fixed here)

1. **No JSON-LD on /pricing, /how-it-works, /features, /blog.** Add `WebPage` + `BreadcrumbList`, and `Offer` items on /pricing that match llms.txt prices. Low effort.
2. **Meta descriptions over 165 characters** on /resume-templates pages (185-223), /resume, /tools, /looks. Google rewrites them. Trim template descriptions to one sentence plus the test stamp.
3. **Blog posts average 857 words.** Ranking pages for generic resume queries are usually far longer and include examples. Expand the posts that already get impressions first (academic CV, German CV, how-to-tailor), not the ones at position 60+.
4. **Generic posts with no chance this year** ("how to write a resume", "resume action verbs"): keep them for internal links, but stop writing more in that style.
5. **Off-site authority is near zero.** Without links, even good posts sit on page 3. The directory and community work in `DAILY_LOOP_2026-11-05.md` is what moves this.
6. **Comparison pages** ("free alternative to Zety / Resume.io / Canva resume") are the highest-intent pages a resume builder can have. They wait on the owner's decision to name competitors. Prices on them must be checked live on the day of publishing.

## Programmatic SEO assessment

Existing sets: 42 template pages (about 5,300 words each, strong), 46 resume examples (about 750 words, FAQ, good), 15 keyword pages (now enriched). Templates and examples are the right playbooks for this product.

Next pSEO candidates, in order:
1. **Resume headline and objective by role** (/resume-headline/[role]): the new freshers posts test demand first. Build the set only if those posts get impressions within 4 weeks.
2. **More resume examples for India-heavy roles** (B.Com fresher, mechanical fresher, BPO, bank PO, teacher): reuse the example engine; every page needs a real sample render, never a placeholder.
3. **Do not** build "[role] resume in [city]" pages: no unique data per page, classic doorway risk.

## AI search

- Robots allows OAI-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, Claude-SearchBot and Googlebot (no AI-specific blocks).
- llms.txt states brand names, disambiguation from Interfolio Dossier, prices, template testing method and page links. Keep it in sync when the template count or prices change.
- ChatGPT sent about 14% of users on 11-02 to 11-03. Bing indexing feeds ChatGPT and Copilot: confirm the site is verified in Bing Webmaster Tools.
- New posts lead each section with a 40-60 word direct answer and carry FAQPage schema, which helps extraction by answer engines.
