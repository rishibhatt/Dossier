## Why signup leaks (found in code)

1. **The Brevo IP block failed every confirmation email** until it was fixed on 2026-11-03. Most of the 2-day window ran with signup emails failing.
2. **PDF download requires sign-in** (`ResumeEditor.tsx` `onDownload`). The person who just built a resume is sent to sign up at the moment they want the file.
3. **Email confirmation loses the resume.** Drafts live in `localStorage` on the device (`src/features/resume/lib/drafts.ts`). The confirm link often opens in the mail app's own browser, a different browser, or a different device, where the draft does not exist.
4. **Confirmation also loses the destination.** `signUpWithPassword` sets `emailRedirectTo` to `/auth/callback` without the `next` path, so a confirmed user lands on `/dashboard` instead of back in the editor with `?download=1`.
5. **No `sign_up` or `login` event is tracked**, so none of the tools can count signups.

## Ranked actions

### Fix now (signup leak)
1. **Turn off "Confirm email" in Supabase** (Authentication → Providers → Email). Signup then returns a session at once. The action already redirects to `next` when a session exists, so the person goes back to the editor and the PDF downloads. The disposable-email block and rate limits stay. Turn confirmation back on later as a soft "verify your email" banner if abuse appears.
2. If confirmation stays on: pass `next` into `emailRedirectTo`, and save the draft to the account before sending the person to their inbox.
3. Make **Continue with Google** the first button in the download sign-in dialog. It never leaves the page and needs no email.
4. Track `sign_up` (with method) and `login` in `track()`, then mark these as key events in GA4 (Admin → Events): `sign_up`, `editor_start`, `pdf_downloaded`, `portfolio_cta_click`, `checkout_started`, `checkout_paid`.

### Double down (what already works)
5. **AI search:** 14% of users come from ChatGPT. Check that `llms.txt`, clear product facts, and the pricing page are readable by AI agents (run the `ai-seo` skill).
6. **Brand query:** "dossier resume" at position 6. Add Organization schema with `sameAs` links to the brand social profiles, and use "Dossier Resume" in the home title.
7. **Country CV cluster:** write the UK, Ireland, Australia and Canada posts, each linking into `/resume/new` with a matching template.
8. **Shared portfolios:** `/u/` and `/p/` pages already bring visits. Keep the "Made with Dossier" link and the share step after publish.

### Clean the data

10. Pricing gets 15 users at 28 s and no checkout. Re-check after the signup fix: nobody can pay before they can sign in.
