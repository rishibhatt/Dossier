Checked 2026-10-23 from public pages. User counts are each vendor's own claim, unverified. Zety, MyPerfectResume, LiveCareer, Teal and Canva blocked or timed out; their figures come from search snippets of their own pages and are marked [snippet].

Takeaway for Dossier: most builders charge for the PDF (Resume.io, Zety, Resume Genius, LiveCareer, MyPerfectResume). FlowCV and Rezi are the free-PDF competitors. Template counts above 100 are mostly colour and filter variants of a few dozen layouts.

## Labour data (who is looking for work)

- US BLS OES May 2025 largest occupations: home health and personal care aides 4.31M, retail salespersons 3.90M, fast food and counter workers 3.85M, general and operations managers 3.50M, registered nurses 3.38M, cashiers 3.09M, service sales reps 2.37M, waiters 2.27M, software developers 1.69M, accountants 1.45M, nursing assistants 1.45M, cooks 1.41M, elementary teachers 1.39M.
- US JOLTS (Aug 2026, preliminary): most hires in trade, transport and utilities (1.09M); highest hires rate in leisure and hospitality (5.3%).
- BLS 2025–35 most new jobs: home health aides +847k, stockers +251k, fast food +223k, RNs +195k, operations managers +181k, software developers +175k, cooks +171k, health services managers +155k.
- India (Naukri JobSpeak 2026): insurance +21%, BPO/ITES +15%, real estate +12%, healthcare +11%; fresher hiring +11–17%; AI/ML roles +34%.
- No independent survey breaks resume-builder users down by stage or industry. Vendors' student and first-job pages suggest students and entry-level users are a core segment; this is an inference.

## What we did with it (2026-10-23)

1. 30 new templates (R-013 to R-042) from 15 ATS-safe patterns, aimed at the largest segments: retail and customer service (Counter, Crew), hourly and warehouse work (Shift), trades and construction (Site, Field), nursing and care (Bedside, Ward), admin and government (Docket, Civic), finance and banking (Folio, Quarter, Tally), law (Brief), teaching (Lecture), data and IT (Signal, Kernel), product and project (Cadence, Transit), sales (Pitch), students (Campus), medicine and labs (Clinic, Bench), social work (Mercy), design and fashion (Atelier, Thread, Notebook, Range), consulting (Atlas), European timeline CVs (Margin).
2. 18 new sample people for the gaps: customer service (India BPO), retail sales, admin assistant, medical assistant, home health aide, barista, chef, warehouse associate, truck driver, electrician, construction supervisor (UAE), data scientist (India), civil engineer, IT support, real estate agent, security officer, pharmacist (UAE), social worker. 46 samples in total; every one is a verification fixture.
3. Still open: home health aides and caregivers are the largest US occupation and thin on competitor sites; more care, food service and driver example pages are a good SEO gap. India fresher and BPO formats are growing.
