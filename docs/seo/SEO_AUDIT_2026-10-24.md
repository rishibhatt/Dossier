# SEO audit: dossier-cv.com (2026-10-24)

Evidence: live fetch of every sitemap URL, page checks of 11 key pages, search and backlink checks (details at the end). Not available: Google Search Console data, PageSpeed field data (the API quota was exhausted).

## Why nobody arrives from Google yet

1. **Google has indexed the site, but only a handful of pages and with old titles, and nothing links to it.** The owner's Google `site:dossier-cv.com` search (2026-10-24) shows the home page (still titled "Dossier: your resume, set as a portfolio website"), `/faq`, `/blog` and `/build`. The resume pages added on 2026-10-20 aren't visible there yet. Being indexed is not the same as ranking: with no backlinks or mentions anywhere we checked (Product Hunt, Hacker News, GitHub, directories), Google has no reason to rank a two-week-old domain above established sites. This is the main cause.
2. **"Resume builder" and "portfolio builder" are head terms owned by very strong domains.** Resume Genius, ResumeBuilder.com, Zety, Hostinger, Squarespace and Wix have years of links. A new domain will not rank for those two phrases in the next few months whatever the page says. The realistic path is brand first, then long-tail queries where our pages are unique, then authority, and only then head terms.
3. **The technical setup is mostly sound**, so it isn't what blocks traffic:
   - robots.txt allows everything public and names the sitemap.
   - The sitemap has 92 URLs, and all return 200.
   - Canonicals are absolute and self-referencing, and every page has one H1 and its own title and description.
   - JSON-LD is on most key pages.
   - HTTP and www redirect to the apex.
